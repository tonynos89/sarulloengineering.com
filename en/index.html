About me

I'm an engineer and I run family businesses in the olive pomace oil extraction industry.

Every day I work with plants, shifts, energy costs and margins. To really understand where the money was going, I built my own management control software, which we now use to make decisions in the company. Now I'm bringing this experience to other small and medium businesses and to machine builders.

  • Hands-on experience running production plants
  • Management control software built and used in my own company
  • I work in Italian and English

From data to decisions

I work with owners of small and medium businesses, and with machine builders who want to offer their customers reports and analysis of how their plant is running.

  1. I collect data from your machines

    I connect PLCs, energy meters and the systems already on site, and bring the data together in one place, clean and reliable.

  2. I analyse it and make it readable

    Automatic reports and AI analysis: what went well, what didn't, and what energy costs for each unit produced.

  3. I feed it into management control

    Costs, margins and company performance on one dashboard, up to date and clear even for non-technical people.
