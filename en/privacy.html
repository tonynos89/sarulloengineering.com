Privacy policy

This page explains what data sarulloengineering.com processes and how. Last updated: October 2026.

Data controller

Antonino Sarullo, Sarullo Engineering, Italy. For any request: antonino@sarulloengineering.com.

Cookies and analytics

This site uses no cookies, no analytics or profiling tools, and loads nothing from external services: fonts, images and scripts are hosted on the site itself. Your browser only stores the language you choose, so you see the right version next time; this stays on your device and is not sent to anyone.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). Like any web server, GitHub may log technical data about visits, such as IP addresses, for security and to run the service. GitHub processes this data under its own privacy statement.

Email

If you write to me, I use your address and the information you send only to reply and, if you wish, to start working together. The legal basis is responding to your request (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). I keep messages for as long as needed to manage our contact and never pass them on to third parties.

Your rights

You can ask at any time to access, correct or delete your data, restrict its processing or object to it, by writing to the address above (Art. 15–22 GDPR). You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Italian data protection authority (garanteprivacy.it) or the authority in your country.
